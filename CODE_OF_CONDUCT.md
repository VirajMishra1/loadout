# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Expected:** constructive feedback, respectful disagreement, focusing on
what is best for the project.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing others'
private information, or any conduct that would be considered inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the
contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include credentials,
private source code, or unrelated personal data. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and fairly, with confidentiality respected to the extent possible.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Temporary
or permanent bans may be applied when conduct is threatening, harassing, or
repeated after a warning.
